What is a word counter?

A word counter measures how many words are in your text. It is useful for blog posts, articles, assignments, social media content and SEO writing.

How does this word counter work?

Type or paste text into the box above. The tool counts words, characters and sentences automatically and estimates reading time at about 200 words per minute.

Is my text stored?

No. This page uses JavaScript in your browser to calculate the results. Your text is not uploaded to a server by the word counter.
