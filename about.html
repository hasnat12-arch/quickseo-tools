QuickSEO Tools is a small collection of free, browser-based utilities built for bloggers, content writers and website owners who need quick answers without signing up for anything or sending their text to a server.

Why we built this

Checking word count, keyword density, or meta tag length usually means opening a paid SEO suite or pasting your draft into a random website. QuickSEO Tools keeps things simple: every tool runs entirely inside your own browser, nothing you type is uploaded or stored anywhere.

What you'll find here

A word and character counter, a keyword density checker, a meta title/description length checker, a case converter, a URL slug generator, and a password generator — all on one page, free to use as often as you like.

Who runs this site

QuickSEO Tools is an independent, self-hosted project run by Hasnat. You can reach out any time via the contact page.
